Projects

📄 Project 1: Journal Manuscript Research
🟢 Completed
Project Title: Socioeconomic and Health-System Correlates of Cesarean Delivery in Bangladesh

A comprehensive study using 5,052 nationally representative birth records. Integrated survey-weighted generalized estimating equations (GEE) with an explainable Stacking Ensemble (Test ROC-AUC: 0.941) and SHAP values to evaluate how healthcare-system structures and socioeconomic inequalities drive the 44.7% cesarean prevalence.

📌 Data Source: BDHS 2022 (Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey)
Tools Used: R RStudio GEE SHAP

📄 Project 2: Conference Presentation
🟢 Completed
Project Title: Socioeconomic Determinants of Cesarean Section Delivery: An EFAST 2026 Conference Paper

Presented at the International Conference on Emerging Frontiers in Advanced Sciences and Technologies (EFAST 2026). Developed a 3-stage analytical framework evaluating 6 ML classifiers to predict delivery context risk, proving private health facility usage as the strongest predictor (Cramér's V = 0.763) alongside intensive antenatal care.

📌 Data Source: BDHS 2022 (Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey)
Tools Used: Python Scikit-Learn Jupyter Notebook Youden's J-Index
