Project Title: Socioeconomic and Health-System Correlates of Cesarean Delivery in Bangladesh

Population-level research using 5,052 nationally representative birth records from BDHS 2022, integrating survey-weighted GEE, machine learning, Stacking Ensemble, and SHAP-based Explainable AI (XAI) to investigate socioeconomic and health-system factors associated with cesarean delivery.

📄 Project 2: Predictive Machine Learning & Explainable AI Research
🟢 Completed
Project Title: Socioeconomic Determinants of Cesarean Section Delivery: An EFAST 2026 Conference Paper

Developed a 3-stage analytical framework integrating survey-weighted GEE, 6 machine learning classifiers, and SHAP-based Explainable AI to identify key socioeconomic and healthcare-system determinants of cesarean delivery, with private health facility usage showing the strongest association (Cramér's V = 0.763).

📌 Data Source: BDHS 2022 (Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey)
🛠️Tools & Techniques: Python Scikit-Learn Jupyter Notebook Machine Learning Stratified Cross-Validation Explainable AI (XAI)
